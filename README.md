# Dishes Rich With Culture and Flavor: The Philippines' Cuisines

An interactive web project created for a school challenge showcasing traditional Filipino dishes, their history, preparation, and cultural significance.

---

## Project Overview

This website serves as a digital showcase celebrating Filipino heritage through food. It highlights 9 iconic traditional dishes, exploring how survival techniques, regional climates, trade history, and colonial influences shaped Philippine culinary identity.

---

## Featured Dishes & Cultural Highlights

1. **Chicken Adobo**
   * Tagline: Savory, tangy braised chicken in soy and vinegar.
   * Cultural Story: Originates from pre-colonial preservation methods where islanders used native palm vinegar and sea salt to keep meat from spoiling in the tropical heat.

2. **Sinigang na Baboy**
   * Tagline: Sour and savory tamarind pork soup.
   * Cultural Story: Highlights the Filipino palate's preference for sour broth (asim), traditionally flavored with indigenous sour fruits like tamarind, kamias, or guava.

3. **Kare-Kare**
   * Tagline: Rich peanut stew with oxtail and vegetables.
   * Cultural Story: Traces historical ties to Indian Sepoy troops who settled in Rizal during the 18th-century British occupation of Manila, combining local ingredients with peanut sauces.

4. **Chicken Tinola**
   * Tagline: Warm and soothing ginger chicken soup.
   * Cultural Story: A traditional comforting broth dish centered on ginger and garlic, celebrated for its healing warmth and simple, nourishing flavors.

5. **Bicol Express**
   * Tagline: Fiery, creamy pork stew simmered in coconut milk and chillies.
   * Cultural Story: Hails from the Bicol region—known for its abundant coconut palms and spicy cuisine—balancing native siling labuyo with rich coconut cream.

6. **Lumpiang Shanghai**
   * Tagline: Crispy fried spring rolls filled with seasoned pork.
   * Cultural Story: Inspired by spring rolls brought by early Chinese traders, adapted locally using minced pork and wrapped thin for family celebrations and fiestas.

7. **Halo-Halo**
   * Tagline: The ultimate shaved ice dessert with mixed sweets.
   * Cultural Story: Evolved from pre-war Japanese shaved-ice treats (kakigori), enriched over time with Filipino sweet preserves, leche flan, and ube halaya.

8. **Beef Kaldereta**
   * Tagline: Hearty beef stew with tomato sauce, liver spread, and vegetables.
   * Cultural Story: Influenced by Spanish colonial stews (from caldero, meaning cauldron), enriched with liver paste and bell peppers for special gatherings.

9. **Lechon**
   * Tagline: Crispy-skinned whole roasted pig seasoned with aromatic herbs.
   * Cultural Story: The centerpiece of major Filipino celebrations, slow-roasted over charcoal with lemongrass, garlic, and bay leaves stuffed inside.

## Design Features

* Bamboo side borders to represent traditional Filipino architecture and materials.
* A textured background showcasing heritage patterns.
* A ripped paper quote section at the bottom featuring a quote by food historian Doreen Gamboa Fernandez on food, culture, and national identity.
* Interactive modal popups that open when clicking a dish card to display ingredients, preparation steps, nutritional values, and historical stories.
